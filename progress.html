Understanding Your Progress Dashboard

How to read and use your data to improve

This progress dashboard is designed around the self-regulated learning competency that sits at the heart of Kenya's Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) as defined by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD). Self-regulated learners are students who actively monitor their own understanding, set goals based on evidence, and adjust their study strategies accordingly. This dashboard gives you the data you need to do exactly that — without requiring any technical knowledge or external tools.

Your Overall Accuracy is the single most diagnostic number on this page. An accuracy rate above 80% in a given topic suggests you have genuinely mastered the concepts being tested. An accuracy rate below 60% is a clear signal that there is a conceptual gap — a piece of reasoning that has not yet clicked — and that you should return to the lesson, re-read the worked examples, or Ask Mwalimu for a targeted explanation before attempting more practice problems in that topic.

Your Day Streak measures how many consecutive days you have engaged with the platform. Educational research consistently shows that distributed practice — studying a little every day — produces far stronger long-term retention than massed practice (cramming). Even solving just 5 problems per day maintains your mathematical fluency and builds the automaticity required for the speed demanded in KCSE Paper 1 and Paper 2 examinations.

The Topic Mastery bars below provide a visual map of your curriculum coverage across all 13 CBC Senior Secondary mathematics topics. For KCSE examination preparation, strong performance across all 13 topics is essential — the examination tests the full breadth of the Senior Secondary mathematics syllabus, and neglecting even one topic area can cost significant marks across multiple questions.

How Your Progress is Saved — Privacy Explained

No server, no account, no personal data collected

Ronny Best Mathematics is committed to student privacy. Unlike many educational platforms that require account registration and store your learning data on external servers, this platform saves all progress data exclusively on your own device using the browser's local storage technology — a standard, secure, device-side storage mechanism built into every modern web browser. No personal information (no name, email, phone number, or academic records) is ever transmitted to any server or shared with any third party.

✅
No Login Required

Your progress saves automatically. No account, email, or password is ever needed at any point on this platform.

🎮
Automatic Tracking

Every practice problem and brain game is recorded instantly. Progress bars and stats update in real time after each answer.

📱
Device-Specific

Your data lives in the browser on your current device. To keep your progress, use the same browser and device consistently.

⚠️
Clearing Browser Data

If you clear your browser's cookies and site data, your progress will be permanently deleted. This action cannot be reversed.
