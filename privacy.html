Privacy Policy

BestRonny Math Learning Platform

Effective Date: January 2024 | Designed for Learners of All Ages

🎓 Educational Mission

BestRonny is a mathematics learning platform designed to help students, teachers, and lifelong learners master mathematical concepts. We are committed to creating a safe, educational online environment.

1. Information We Collect

As an educational website, we collect minimal information to enhance the learning experience:

  • Educational Progress Data: Anonymous data on which math topics users access (for improving content)
  • Technical Information: Browser type, device information (for compatibility optimization)
  • Usage Patterns: Which math lessons are most popular (to improve our educational materials)
  • Ad-related Data: Google AdSense may collect anonymized data for age-appropriate advertising

2. Safe & Educational Advertising

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 For Parents & Teachers

We use Google AdSense with education-friendly settings:

  • Ads are filtered to be child-safe and education-appropriate
  • No personalized data collection from minors
  • Advertising supports keeping our math resources free for students
  • We use non-intrusive ad placements that don't disrupt learning

Google AdSense for Education: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements that help fund free math education. Google uses cookies to serve age-appropriate, education-relevant ads.

🔒 Student Privacy: We have configured our AdSense settings to prioritize educational content and minimize data collection from student users.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. COPPA Compliance (Children's Online Privacy Protection)

BestRonny is COPPA-compliant:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Advertising is filtered for age-appropriate content
  • Parents can contact us to review or delete any information
  • We use anonymous analytics to improve math lessons without identifying individual users

Note: Our math content is designed to be accessible to learners of all ages, including children, with appropriate privacy protections.

4. How We Use Information for Education

Data helps us improve math education:

  • Identify which math concepts need better explanations
  • Optimize lesson difficulty levels
  • Create new math tutorials based on user needs
  • Make the website more accessible on different devices
  • Keep the platform free through responsible advertising

5. Contact for Privacy Concerns

For students, parents, or teachers with privacy questions:

  • Website: bestronny.netlify.app
  • Purpose: Mathematics Education Platform
  • Data Use: Educational improvement only
  • Advertising: Education-appropriate ads only

We are committed to maintaining a safe learning environment while keeping math education accessible to everyone.
