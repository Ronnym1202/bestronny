Our Educational Mission

BestRonny is a free mathematics learning platform designed to help students, teachers, and lifelong learners master mathematical concepts. We are committed to maintaining a safe, transparent, and privacy-respecting online environment for learners of all ages.

We keep our platform completely free by displaying education-appropriate advertisements through Google AdSense. This policy explains exactly what data is collected, why, and how you can control it.

2

Information We Collect

As an educational website, we collect the minimum data necessary to operate and improve the platform:

  • Educational Usage Data: Anonymous data on which math topics and games are accessed, used solely to improve content quality.
  • Technical Information: Browser type and device information collected for compatibility optimisation — not linked to any individual.
  • Ad-Related Data: Google AdSense may collect anonymised data (via cookies and web beacons) to serve age-appropriate advertisements. See Section 4 for full details.
  • Local Game Progress: Your game scores and levels are saved using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, location, or any personally identifiable information. No account or login is required.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we process personal data only where we have a lawful basis under GDPR Article 6:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): We obtain your consent before placing advertising cookies via Google AdSense. You may withdraw this consent at any time — see Section 6 below.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Anonymous, aggregated analytics to understand which math content is most useful — no personal data is involved.
  • Legal Obligation: Where required, we may process data to comply with applicable law.
Your right: You may withdraw consent for advertising cookies at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Google AdSense, Cookies & Advertising

Our website uses Google AdSense (Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) to display advertisements that fund free math education.

  • Cookies: Google AdSense places text files ("cookies") on your device to analyse usage and serve relevant ads.
  • Web Beacons: Invisible pixel graphics may be used alongside cookies to measure ad performance.
  • Data Transmitted: Information generated (including your anonymised IP address) is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers, which may be in the United States.
  • Google's Use: Google evaluates usage, compiles reports for us, and may share data with third parties where required by law or where those parties process data on Google's behalf.
  • Ad Placement: Ads appear only in the header, footer, sidebars, and between-game areas — never inside active game interfaces, in compliance with Google AdSense placement policies.
Manage Google advertising data:

Your Consent & How to Withdraw It

When you first visit this website, a cookie consent banner asks for your permission before any advertising cookies are placed. You can freely accept or decline non-essential cookies.

How to withdraw or change your consent at any time:

  1. Clear browser cookies — go to your browser's Settings → Privacy → Clear cookies and site data.
  2. Adjust browser settings — most browsers let you block third-party cookies under Settings → Privacy.
  3. Opt out of Google personalised ads — visit adssettings.google.com.
  4. Use a browser extension — tools like uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger block advertising cookies automatically.
  5. Revisit this page — refresh to see the consent banner again after clearing cookies.
Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of any processing already carried out before withdrawal. It will not affect your ability to use the maths games — game progress is stored locally and does not depend on advertising cookies.

COPPA Compliance & Child Safety

BestRonny is COPPA-compliant under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (USA) and aligned with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.
  • No personal data is knowingly collected from children under 13.
  • Advertising is filtered for child-safe, age-appropriate content at all times.
  • No targeted advertising is directed at users identified as minors.
  • Parents and guardians may contact us to review or request deletion of any information relating to their child.
  • All anonymous analytics used to improve lessons do not identify individual users of any age.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.

7

Safe & Education-Friendly Advertising

For parents and teachers, here is how we ensure advertising does not disrupt the learning environment:

  • Ads are filtered to display child-safe and education-appropriate content only.
  • No pop-ups, auto-play video ads, or intrusive formats are used.
  • Ads are placed in clearly separated areas (header, footer, sidebars) — never overlapping or inside game interfaces.
  • All ad slots include "Advertisement" labels to clearly distinguish them from educational content.
  • We do not compensate users for clicking ads, and we never ask users to click ads.
  • Revenue from advertising goes entirely toward keeping all math content free.
8

Local Storage & Game Progress

Our math games save your progress (scores, levels, streaks) using your browser's localStorage feature — a standard browser technology.

  • Data saved includes: game level, high scores, and streak counts.
  • This data stays entirely on your device — it is never sent to our servers or to any third party.
  • It is not a cookie and is not accessible by Google AdSense or any advertiser.
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.
No account needed. Your game progress is yours alone, stored privately on your own device.
