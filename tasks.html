How to Use This Practice Page

All Levels

This practice page is designed to help high school and university students master mathematics through active problem-solving. Unlike passive reading, solving problems yourself is the most effective way to build genuine mathematical understanding and exam confidence.

Each time you click Generate Problem, a new randomised question is created from a bank of carefully designed problem types. Enter your answer, then click Check to see whether you are correct — and a full step-by-step solution is always shown so you can learn from every attempt, right or wrong.

You can also type any mathematics question in plain English into the Ask Any Math Question box, and our built-in solver will work through it for you with detailed working. Still stuck? Use the Ask Mwalimu community board to get a personal reply.

Topics Covered in This Practice Section

All Levels

The practice generator covers all 13 core areas of mathematics, from A-Level to first-year university. Each topic includes multiple problem types with fully worked solutions.

d/dx

Differentiation

Power rule, product rule, quotient rule, and chain rule. Polynomials, trig, exponentials, and logarithms.

  • Power rule: d/dx(xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹
  • Product, quotient, and chain rules
  • Trig & exponential derivatives
  • Stationary points & turning points
∫

Integration

Indefinite and definite integration, reverse power rule, trig, exponentials, and natural logarithms.

  • Indefinite integrals & constant of integration
  • Definite integrals & Fundamental Theorem
  • Integration of sin, cos, eˣ, and 1/x
  • Applying limits to evaluate areas
[ ]

Matrices

Determinants, inverses, matrix multiplication, eigenvalues, and transpose operations.

  • 2×2 determinants: det = ad − bc
  • Testing for inverse existence
  • Matrix multiplication & column vectors
  • Eigenvalues & characteristic equations
→

Vectors

Magnitude, dot product, cross product, unit vectors, and angle between vectors in 2D and 3D.

  • Magnitude: |v| = √(x²+y²+z²)
  • Dot product: a·b = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ + a₃b₃
  • Perpendicular & parallel vectors
  • Unit vectors & direction
𝑥

Algebra

Linear equations, quadratics, simultaneous equations, inequalities, and factorisation.

  • Solve ax + b = c
  • Quadratic formula & discriminant
  • Simultaneous equations (2 variables)
  • Linear inequalities
△

Trigonometry

Evaluate trig functions, solve trig equations, sine rule, cosine rule, and Pythagoras.

  • sin, cos, tan, sec, cosec, cot
  • Solve sin(x) = k, cos(x) = k
  • Sine & cosine rules
  • Pythagorean theorem
σ

Statistics

Mean, median, mode, range, standard deviation, probability, and combinatorics.

  • Mean, median, mode
  • Variance & standard deviation
  • Probability calculations
  • Permutations & combinations
∑

Sequences & Series

Arithmetic and geometric sequences, nth term, sum formulas, and convergence.

  • Arithmetic: aₙ = a + (n−1)d
  • Geometric: aₙ = arⁿ⁻¹
  • Sum of AP and GP
  • Sum to infinity (|r| < 1)
i

Complex Numbers

Modulus, argument, conjugate, arithmetic of complex numbers in Cartesian form.

  • Modulus: |z| = √(a²+b²)
  • Argument: arg(z) = arctan(b/a)
  • Conjugate & division
  • Multiply complex numbers
lim

Limits

Direct substitution, indeterminate forms, L'Hôpital's rule, and limits at infinity.

  • Direct substitution
  • 0/0 indeterminate — factoring
  • L'Hôpital's rule
  • Limits as x → ∞
□

Areas of Shapes

Triangles, circles, trapezoids, sectors, and surface areas of 3D solids.

  • A = ½ × base × height (triangle)
  • A = πr² (circle)
  • A = ½(a + b) × h (trapezoid)
  • SA = 6s² (cube)
⬡

Volumes of Solids

Cylinders, spheres, cones, rectangular boxes, and composite solids.

  • V = πr²h (cylinder)
  • V = (4/3)πr³ (sphere)
  • V = (1/3)πr²h (cone)
  • V = l × w × h (box)
log

Logarithms

Logarithm laws, solving log and exponential equations, change of base, and natural log.

  • log(ab) = log a + log b
  • log(aⁿ) = n log a
  • Solve eˣ = k → x = ln(k)
  • Change-of-base formula

Sample Worked Problems

Secondary / A-Level

Study these fully worked examples before attempting the practice generator.

Differentiation

Find the derivative of \( f(x) = 3x^4 - 2x^2 + 5x - 7 \)

Answer: \( f'(x) = 12x^3 - 4x + 5 \)
  1. \( \frac{d}{dx}(3x^4) = 12x^3 \) — power rule
  2. \( \frac{d}{dx}(-2x^2) = -4x \)
  3. \( \frac{d}{dx}(5x) = 5 \), \( \frac{d}{dx}(-7) = 0 \)
Integration

Evaluate \( \int (2x^3 - 3x + 4)\, dx \)

Answer: \( \tfrac{1}{2}x^4 - \tfrac{3}{2}x^2 + 4x + C \)
  1. \(\int 2x^3\,dx = \frac{1}{2}x^4\)
  2. \(\int -3x\,dx = -\frac{3}{2}x^2\)
  3. \(\int 4\,dx = 4x\) — always add \(+ C\)
Matrices

Find \( \det\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&5\end{pmatrix} \)

Answer: \( (1)(5) - (2)(3) = 5 - 6 = -1 \)
  1. Formula: det = ad − bc
  2. a=1, b=2, c=3, d=5
  3. det = (1×5) − (2×3) = −1

2

When you check your answer, read through the full step-by-step solution carefully — not just the final answer.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Exam Tips

Based on years of teaching experience, these are the most frequent errors students make in each area.

d/dx   Differentiation

Forgetting to bring the power down before reducing the index. Also, forgetting that the derivative of a constant is always zero.

∫   Integration

Forgetting to add the constant of integration + C in indefinite integrals — an automatic mark deduction in most marking schemes.

[ ]   Matrices

Getting the determinant formula backwards: it is always ad − bc, not ad + bc. A singular matrix (det = 0) has no inverse.

→   Vectors

Confusing magnitude (a scalar) with the vector itself. For perpendicularity, the dot product must equal exactly zero.

△   Trigonometry

Using degrees when the formula requires radians. In sector/arc formulas, the angle must always be in radians.

∑   Sequences

Confusing the formula for the nth term with the sum formula. For a GP sum to infinity, |r| must be less than 1.

□   Areas

Using diameter instead of radius in A = πr². For the trapezoid, both parallel sides must be included.

⬡   Volumes

Forgetting the factor of 1/3 for cones and pyramids. Always state units³ in your final answer.
