Our Story

How It All Started

Ronny Best was born out of a simple but powerful frustration: quality math education was too expensive, too inaccessible, and too intimidating for most students in Kenya and across Africa.

After more than a decade of teaching mathematics to high school and college students, Ronny Mwenda noticed a pattern — students weren't struggling because they lacked intelligence. They were struggling because they didn't have access to clear, step-by-step explanations they could revisit at their own pace.

This platform was created to change that. Every lesson, example, and practice problem on this site is the result of years of classroom experience, refined to be as clear and approachable as possible.

Our Mission

Mathematics for Every Student

We believe mathematics is not an elite subject reserved for the naturally gifted. It is a skill — and like any skill, it can be learned with the right guidance, patience, and practice.

Our mission is to provide every student — regardless of their background, school, or financial situation — with the same quality of mathematics instruction they would get from a private tutor. For free. Forever.

Mathematics isn't about memorising formulas — it's about understanding patterns and developing problem-solving skills that last a lifetime.

— Ronny Best, Founder

What We Offer

A Complete Learning Experience

From foundational algebra to university-level calculus and complex numbers, Ronny Best Mathematics covers the full spectrum of high school and early college mathematics. Every topic includes detailed explanations, worked examples, practice problems, and real-world applications.

We also offer interactive brain games, a progress tracker, and achievement badges to keep learning engaging and motivating. Our content is specifically aligned with East African curricula while remaining globally relevant.

Our Commitment

Always Free, Always Improving

This platform will always be free. We are supported by responsible advertising that allows us to keep the lights on without ever charging students. Every lesson you read helps keep this resource alive for the next student who needs it.

We are constantly updating content, fixing errors, and adding new topics based on feedback from students and teachers. If you spot anything that could be improved, we always want to hear from you.
